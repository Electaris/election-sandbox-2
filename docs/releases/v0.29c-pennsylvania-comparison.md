# v0.29C: Pennsylvania comparison proof

Date: 2026-09-07

Version: 0.29.2

The reference compiler now has a reproducible real Pennsylvania candidate: all 3,879 Census blocks and 50 VTDs in Adams County. The county pilot matches the admitted VTD foundation exactly across all five demographic groups and total VAP, at every local unit. Its 83,005 VAP and 60,650 linked 2024 ballots provide no coverage or composition advantage.

**Decision: retain the existing VTD foundation.** This is a bounded comparison proof, not statewide admission or a runtime migration.

- Added checksum-pinned Census archives, extracted-member hashes, normalized source geometry, and offline reproduction.
- Used original Census NAD83 geometry through explicit EPSG:4269 support; no simplification or datum relabeling.
- Emitted a canonical candidate artifact and complete block trace outside application assets.
- Compared every VTD's counts, group shares, availability, source methods, and result links.
- Preserved the statewide off-map inventory, including Philadelphia and statewide reconciliation buckets.
- Recorded size, source and output fingerprints, adversarial tests, and the no-migration verdict.

The candidate adds build-time auditability. It retains the 2020 demographic and terrain vintages and the existing 2024 result linkage. The county's success does not demonstrate urban, exact-cycle, split, or statewide replacement quality.
